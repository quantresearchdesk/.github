## About QRD

**Quant Research Desk (QRD)** is an independent quantitative research collaboration working across systematic trading, empirical financial research, portfolio construction, market microstructure and the engineering infrastructure needed for reproducible experiments.

Our work aims to separate **plausible economic mechanisms** from **patterns that only look persuasive in historical data**. That requires careful testing, transparent assumptions and attention to the limits of the evidence.

> **Research principle:** A strong backtest is a hypothesis to investigate, not proof of a durable edge.

## How we approach research

```text
Question / economic hypothesis
              |
              v
Data sourcing and quality checks
              |
              v
Research design and baseline models
              |
              v
Out-of-sample and robustness testing
              |
              v
Trading-cost and implementation analysis
              |
              v
Results, limitations and reproducibility
```

We regard null or negative results as legitimate research outcomes. We do not represent historical simulations as live performance.

**Public does not mean unrestricted data.** Broker credentials, proprietary strategies, account information, and restricted or licensed market data will not be published.
